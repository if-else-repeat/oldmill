NOTA

India's Election Universe, from Vaishali's ancient republic to 968 million voters.

NOTA is an interactive educational experience built to make India's electoral system easier to understand. Through timelines, historical context, election processes, and democratic institutions, it presents election education in an engaging, visual format, tracing a line from the world's first republic in 600 BCE Vaishali to the record-setting scale of India's elections today.
