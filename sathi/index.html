SĀTHI

Before the yes, let two families truly meet.

SĀTHI is a private, mutual, consent-based space for two families who already have a match in mind. It never searches, never matches, never rates. It simply helps both families understand each other's hopes, expectations, and unspoken questions, before the decision instead of after. Each family answers independently in its own private room, then receives a shared understanding report: where they align, where they differ, and what hasn't been discussed yet. No scores, no verdicts, no public profiles. The decision stays entirely with the families.
